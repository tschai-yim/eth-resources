**Notation:** $A^\dagger$ for $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$.
**Goal:** Generalize the inverse for non-square/singular matrices to solve $Ax \approx b$.

## Case 1: Full Column Rank ($\operatorname{rank}(A)=n$)

- **Context:** Overdetermined systems ($m \ge n$, "Tall" matrix).
- **Definition:** $A^\dagger = (A^\top A)^{-1}A^\top$ .[^def6.4.1]
- **Property:** **Left Inverse** $\to A^\dagger A = I$ .[^prop6.4.2]
- **Function:** $A^\dagger b$ yields the **Least Squares solution** .[^def6.4.1]

## Case 2: Full Row Rank ($\operatorname{rank}(A)=m$)

- **Context:** Underdetermined systems ($m \le n$, "Wide" matrix).
- **Definition:** $A^\dagger = A^\top(AA^\top)^{-1}$ .[^def6.4.3]
- **Property:** **Right Inverse** $\to AA^\dagger = I$ .[^lem6.4.4]
- **Function:** Solves $Ax=b$.
    - Yields unique solution with **minimum norm** ($\min \|x\|$ s.t. $Ax=b$) .[^prop6.4.6]
    - Solution $A^\dagger b$ lies strictly in Row Space $C(A^\top)$ .[^lem6.4.5]

## Case 3: General Rank ($\operatorname{rank}(A)=r$)

- **Context:** Any matrix (using Rank Factorization / CR Decomposition).
- **Definition:** Given decomposition $A=CR$ ($C \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r}$ full col rank, $R \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times n}$ full row rank):
    - $A^\dagger = R^\dagger C^\dagger$ .[^def6.4.7]
    - **Note:** Order reversed ($R^\dagger$ first).
- **General Factorization:** Holds for *any* $A=ST$ where $S, T$ are full rank ($S \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r}, T \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times n}$):
    - $A^\dagger = T^\dagger S^\dagger$ .[^prop6.4.9]
- **Optimization Meaning:** $x = A^\dagger b$ minimizes $\|x\|^2$ among all Least Squares solutions .[^lem6.4.8]

## Geometric Intuition & Operators

- **Left Action ($A^\dagger b$):** Maps $b \in \mathbb{R}^m$ to **Row Space** $C(A^\top)$ (min error, then min norm).
    - Solves $Ax \approx b$.
- **Right Action ($v^\top A^\dagger$):** Maps $v^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times n}$ to **Column Space** $C(A)$ (min error, then min norm).
    - Solves $x^\top A \approx v^\top$ (equivalent to solving $A^\top x \approx v$).
- **Projection Operators:**
    - **$AA^\dagger$:** Projector onto **Column Space** $C(A)$ .[^thm6.4.10]
        - "Closest vector to $b$ that $A$ can produce."
    - **$A^\dagger A$:** Projector onto **Row Space** $C(A^\top)$ .[^thm6.4.10]
        - "Part of $x$ visible to $A$ (ignoring Nullspace component)."

## Fundamental Properties [^thm6.4.10]

- **Moore-Penrose Conditions:**
    1. $AA^\dagger A = A$
    2. $A^\dagger A A^\dagger = A^\dagger$
    3. $(AA^\dagger)^\top = AA^\dagger$ (Symmetric)
    4. $(A^\dagger A)^\top = A^\dagger A$ (Symmetric)
- **Transpose:** $(A^\top)^\dagger = (A^\dagger)^\top$.

[^def6.4.1]: **Definition 6.4.1 (Pseudoinverse for matrices of full column rank).** For $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ with $\operatorname{rank}(A) = n$ we define the pseudo-inverse $A^\dagger \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times m}$ of $A$ as $$A^\dagger = (A^\top A)^{-1}A^\top.$$
[^prop6.4.2]: **Proposition 6.4.2.** For $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ with $\operatorname{rank}(A) = n$, the pseudoinverse $A^\dagger$ is a left inverse of $A$, meaning that $A^\dagger A = I$.
[^def6.4.3]: **Definition 6.4.3 (Pseudoinverse for matrices of full row rank).** For $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ with $\operatorname{rank}(A) = m$ we define the pseudo-inverse $A^\dagger \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times m}$ of $A$ as $$A^\dagger = A^\top(AA^\top)^{-1}.$$
[^lem6.4.4]: **Lemma 6.4.4.** For $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ with $\operatorname{rank}(A) = m$, the pseudoinverse $A^\dagger$ is a right inverse of $A$, meaning that $AA^\dagger = I$.
[^prop6.4.6]: **Proposition 6.4.6.** For a full row rank matrix $A$, the (unique) solution to (11) is given by the vector $\hat{x} = A^\dagger b$.
[^lem6.4.5]: **Lemma 6.4.5.** For any matrix $A$ and a vector $b \in C(A)$, the (unique) solution to (11) is given by the vector $\hat{x} \in C(A^\top)$ that satisfies the constraint $A\hat{x} = b$. *(Note: (11) refers to $\min_{x \in \mathbb{R}^n} \|x\|^2 \text{ s.t. } Ax=b$)*
[^def6.4.7]: **Definition 6.4.7 (Pseudoinverse for all matrices).** For $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ with $\operatorname{rank}(A) = r$ and CR decomposition $A = CR$ we define the pseudoinverse $A^\dagger$ as $$A^\dagger = R^\dagger C^\dagger,$$
[^prop6.4.9]: **Proposition 6.4.9.** For $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, with $\operatorname{rank}(A) = r$, let $S \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r}$ and $T \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times n}$ such that $A = ST$. $$A^\dagger = T^\dagger S^\dagger.$$
[^lem6.4.8]: **Lemma 6.4.8.** Given $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ and a vector $b \in \mathbb{R}^m$, the (unique) solution to $$(12) \quad \min_{x \in \mathbb{R}^n} \|x\|^2 \quad \text{s.t.} \quad A^\top A x = A^\top b,$$ is given by $\hat{x} = A^\dagger b$.
[^thm6.4.10]: **Theorem 6.4.10.** Let $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$.
$AA^\dagger A = A$ and $A^\dagger AA^\dagger = A^\dagger$ and $(A^\top)^\dagger = (A^\dagger)^\top$.
$AA^\dagger$ is symmetric. It is the projection matrix for projection on $C(A)$,
$A^\dagger A$ is symmetric. It is the projection matrix for projection on $C(A^\top)$.
