## What is **Prefetching** in computer architecture?

- **Definition**: Fetching data into the cache hierarchy before an explicit program request.
- **Primary Goal**: Hide high memory latency and completely eliminate **compulsory cache misses**.
- **Granularity**: Typically operates strictly at **cache block granularity**.

## What are the three **Implementation Mechanisms** for prefetching?

- **Software**: **ISA** instructions inserted by the compiler or programmer (highly difficult to optimize dynamically).
- **Hardware**: Dedicated logic that explicitly learns memory access streams.
- **Execution-Based**: A speculative hardware thread that pre-executes code strictly to trigger cache misses.

## How does the **Target Level** dictate cache placement strategy for prefetches?

- **Closer to L1 Cache**: Sees all raw access streams and demands massive bandwidth.
- **Closer to Last Level Cache (LLC)**: Sees filtered access streams and catches broader, system-wide patterns.

## What is **Replacement Skewing** in prefetch cache placement?

- **High-confidence prefetches**: Placed at the **MRU (Most Recently Used)** position.
- **Low-confidence prefetches**: Placed at the **LRU (Least Recently Used)** position to actively prevent **cache pollution** (wasting usable cache space).

## What are the risks of incorrect **Timeliness** in prefetching?

- **Too early**: The block is evicted before the actual program can use it.
- **Too late**: The block arrives late and fails to hide the full memory latency.
- **Tradeoff**: Higher aggressiveness improves timeliness but directly risks hurting accuracy.

## Why does prefetching strictly require **no state recovery** for mispredictions?

- **Correctness Guarantee**: Prefetching is purely opportunistic and does not affect program correctness.
- **Mispredictions**: Wrongly fetched data is simply left unused, avoiding the heavy hardware flushes required by branch mispredictions.

## What is the core **Motivation** for implementing prefetching based on cache utilization?

- Over \\(80\%\\) of cached data remains unused before eviction, making highly accurate prefetching extremely beneficial to system performance.

## What are the three **Primary Metrics** for evaluating prefetcher performance?

- **Accuracy**: Used prefetches / Sent prefetches.
- **Coverage**: Prefetched misses / All misses (\\(100\%\\) accuracy with \\(0\%\\) coverage is completely useless).
- **Timeliness**: On-time prefetches / Used prefetches.

## What are the two **Secondary Metrics** for evaluating prefetcher performance?

- **Bandwidth Consumption**: Consumed memory bandwidth strictly compared with and without prefetching.
- **Cache Pollution**: The creation of extra demand misses caused by useful data being evicted by useless, incorrectly predicted prefetches.

## What are the three **Target Access Patterns** recognized by hardware prefetchers?

- **Simple Regular**: Linear strides (e.g., \\(A, A+N, A+2N\\)).
- **Complex Regular**: Repeating delta sequences (e.g., \\(+2, +3, +4, +2, +3, +4\\)).
- **Irregular**: Linked list traversals, indirect array accesses, or completely random accesses.

## How does a **Stream Prefetcher** operate?

- Sequentially fetches immediately adjacent cache blocks (\\(N+1, N+2...\\)) upon a program accessing block \\(N\\).

## What is the difference between **Instruction-Based** and **Memory-Region-Based** Stride Prefetchers?

- **Instruction-Based**: Indexed by the **Program Counter (PC)**. Tracks the specific stride generated by strictly *one individual memory instruction* over time.
- **Memory-Region-Based**: Indexed by **Cache Block Address**. Tracks strides within a specific physical memory area, detecting patterns even if generated by *multiple interleaved instructions*.

## How does **Path Confidence Based Lookahead** execute complex pattern prefetching?

- Records the historical stride sequence (e.g., \\(\\{-6, 12, 6\\}\\)).
- Bootstraps predictions repeatedly until the cascaded **path confidence** drops strictly below a predefined hardware threshold.

## How do **Self-Optimizing Prefetchers (e.g., Pythia)** operate using Reinforcement Learning?

- Implements Q-learning directly in hardware:
    - **State**: Vector of features (e.g., **PC** + recent address deltas sequence).
    - **Action**: Chosen **prefetch offset** (e.g., \\(-6\\) to \\(+32\\), or \\(0\\) for no prefetch).
    - **Reward**: Calculated via **prefetch usefulness** (accuracy) and system-level feedback (memory bandwidth usage).
- **Benefit**: Consistently yields high performance via dynamic adaptation.

## Why do modern systems utilize **Hybrid Hardware Prefetchers**?

- Real systems deploy multiple specialized prefetchers (e.g., 6-7 per core) simultaneously to explicitly maximize overall **coverage**.

## What are the two main **Multi-Core Challenges** caused by prefetching?

- **Exhausting Shared Bandwidth**: Aggressive prefetching blocks actual demand requests.
- **Interference**: One core's aggressive prefetching generates severe **cache conflicts** and **bus contention**, strictly destroying another core's performance.

## How is **Multi-Core Interference** mitigated in prefetcher hardware?

- Strictly requires dynamic **throttling** and coordinated control of all prefetchers globally across the entire chip.

## What causes a **Full-Window Stall** in an out-of-order processor?

- Long-latency L2/Memory misses block the oldest instruction strictly at the head of the **Reorder Buffer (ROB)**.
- Younger instructions cannot be dispatched, halting the entire processor.
- Expanding the window is impossible due to strict constraints on **power consumption**, **cycle time**, and **complexity**.

## What are the four operational steps of the **Runahead Execution Mechanism**?

1. **Checkpointing**: Saves the architectural state upon encountering a long-latency miss.
2. **Speculation**: Pre-executes future instructions purely to generate fast data prefetches.
3. **Invalidation**: Drops instructions strictly dependent on the missing data (marked **INV**).
4. **Restoration**: Upon data return, restores the checkpoint, flushes the speculative pipeline, and completely resumes normal execution.

## What are the primary advantages of **Runahead Execution**?

- Yields **highly accurate** prefetches because it strictly follows the actual dynamic program path.
- Utilizes idle main thread resources (no wasted hardware contexts).
- Achieves the **Memory-Level Parallelism (MLP)** of a massive **512-entry window** using strictly a **128-entry window**.

## What are the main disadvantages of **Runahead Execution**?

- **Energy Inefficiency**: Executes instructions strictly twice (speculatively, then for real).
- Fails to prefetch **dependent cache misses** (e.g., complex pointer chasing or linked lists).

## How does **Address-Value Delta (AVD) Prediction** enhance Runahead Execution?

- Hardware explicitly predicts the actual *value* of the missing pointer to instantly unblock dependent, nested loads.

## How do **Wrong-Path Events** surprisingly benefit Runahead Execution?

- Branches misspeculated during Runahead execution still often prefetch highly useful cache blocks that apply strictly to the correct execution path.
