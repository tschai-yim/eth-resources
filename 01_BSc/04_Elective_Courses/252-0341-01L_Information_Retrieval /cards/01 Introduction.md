## What is **Information Retrieval (IR)** and where did it originate?

- **Definition**: Focuses on answering user queries.
- **Origin**: Coined by **Calvin Mooers** (1950s).

## What is the **Dewey Decimal System**?

**Numerical library cataloging system** developed by Melvil Dewey.

## What is the evolutionary timeline of **data handling systems** (1960s–2000s)?

- **File Systems**: Dominant in **1960s**.
- **Relational Era (1970s)**: **Edgar Codd** invented **relational databases** (tables).
- **Object Era**: Emerged in **1980s**.
- **NoSQL Era (2000s)**: Web-scaled stores (Key-value, Triple, Column, Document).

## What are examples and characteristics of **Unstructured data**?

- **Examples**:
    - **Text**
    - **Picture (bitmap)**
    - **Audio** / **Video**
    - **Tweets**
- **Characteristics**:
    - **Raw form**, **no explicit structure**.
    - Relies on **implicit features** (e.g., characters, grammar, pixels).

## What is the **Semantic gap**?

Disconnect between:

1. Raw **Representation of Information** (physical data/format).
2. True **Semantic description of information** (human meaning/intent).

## What is the **Knowledge Hierarchy**?

1. **Data** (+ meaning) $\rightarrow$ **Information**.
2. **Information** (+ meaningful application) $\rightarrow$ **Knowledge**.
3. **Knowledge** $\rightarrow$ **Wisdom**.

## What were the **Early IR Systems** (1956-1969)?

- **Memex**: Conceptual mechanical retrieval (Vannevar Bush).
- **SMART (1960s)**: **Gerard Salton**; introduced **vector space model** (linear algebra).
- **IBM STAIRS (1969)**: **Boolean retrieval system** for legal search.

## What is the **Scaling Problem** regarding hardware evolution (1956 vs. 2026)?

Comparison between **IBM RAMAC** (1956) and **ExaDrive** (2026):

- **Capacity** grew drastically ($\uparrow \approx 10^{12}\times$).
- **Throughput** grew much slower ($\uparrow \approx 10^5\times$).
- **Gap**: Capacity **vastly outpaced** throughput.

*Analogy*: Reading a 100 TB drive at human speed = 1287 years.

## What is the solution to the **Scaling Problem** (Capacity vs. Throughput)?

**Parallelization** and **Batch processing** across massive machine clusters (10,000s).
